About Us

DICE QUIBOLOY

"CU" CULTURAL "CU" CUISINE — He studied Culinary Arts in the U.S. and learned how to cook different kinds of food from various countries, including Filipino cuisine. He decided to use the knowledge and dishes he learned abroad, which he had also come to adopt after living overseas for a long time. He combined international dishes with Filipino food to make Filipino cuisine more well-known and to share foreign dishes with Filipinos. That's how he came up with his business, CUCU CLOUD.
